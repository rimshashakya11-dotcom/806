Upload a timesheet a staff member filled in themselves — typed, a photo of a handwritten one, or a scanned PDF (even a multi-page one covering several drivers, one page each). Checks whether the total hours they wrote actually matches IN to OUT minus the break — uses a written break time if there is one, otherwise the standard 30-minute rule. This does not compare against GPS — that's the section above.

Fuel check

Upload a Cardlink (or similar) fuel report — cross-checked against the GPS record to catch fuel bought when a vehicle shows no matching activity

Upload a fuel report (Cardlink PDF, CSV, or Excel) Works directly with a real Cardlink statement or vehicle report PDF, or a CSV/Excel export. Nothing leaves your browser.
How fuel is checked (formulas)
  • Fuel type is read from the Product column (Diesel, Mobil Diesel Efficient, Ultimate Diesel = diesel; Unleaded, Synergy, Premium = petrol). A vehicle's main fuel is the type with the most litres, unless you set it in the Fuel type column.
  • Baseline rate (L/100 km) = median of (litres − extra litres) ÷ km since the previous main-fuel fill × 100, using that vehicle's own history (needs real odometer distance).
  • Expected litres = km ÷ 100 × baseline rate + extra fuel recorded for that fill.
  • Unexplained litres = litres bought − expected litres. Flagged only above max(10 L, 15% of expected).
  • Other-type fuel (petrol on a diesel vehicle, or any fuel on a trailer) is shown separately, left out of the baseline, and not treated as misuse once you record it as extra fuel with a reason.
  • Plug-in hybrids are not judged on litres per 100 km (charging is not recorded). Fleet comparisons use only vehicles of the same fuel type.
