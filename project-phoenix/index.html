Speaker notes · slide 4

Our workforce plan is 20 redundancies, not 40. We start with genuine consultation under the Employment Relations Act and offer voluntary redundancy and redeployment before anyone is selected. The 20 roles are mostly manual testing, junior development, first-line support and some admin layers — the work AI agents now do best. A hiring freeze means another 8 natural leavers aren’t replaced, taking us to 92 by month 12. Thirty people are reskilled into AI-QA and agent orchestration. In Year 2 we hire back specialist AI-ops roles, and we grow again in Year 3. Exit terms sit above NZ market norms because how we treat leavers is how stayers judge us.

06 · Operating model

AI agents build; named humans approve every release

1 · Spec sign-off

Pod lead and client agree scope (human)

2 · AI build

Coding and test agents write and self-test

3 · Human review

Risk-tiered; 100% on security and payments code

4 · Guardian sign-off

A senior engineer certifies and owns the release

5 · Canary release

Staged rollout with automatic rollback

Structure

6 delivery pods (lead, 3–4 engineers, about 3 agents each). The 5 senior engineers become Release Guardians; 8 mid-levels are certified as reviewers by month 9.

Severity-1 escalation

Auto-rollback at once · on-call engineer in 15 min · Guardian in 30 min · CTO calls the client within 1 hour · root-cause review in 5 days.

KiwiSoft · Project Phoenix · 7   |   Accountability: AI never owns a release. The signing Guardian does.

Speaker notes · slide 7

Here’s how the work actually flows. Humans agree the spec with the client. AI coding and testing agents build and self-test — that’s where the doubling of speed comes from. Humans review the code, with 100% review on anything touching security or payments. A senior Release Guardian certifies every release and is accountable for it — AI never owns a release. Then we roll out gradually with automatic rollback. We know only five senior engineers remain, so we’re certifying eight mid-level engineers as reviewers by month 9 and paying retention bonuses to the five. If something breaks, rollback is automatic, a human is on it in 15 minutes and the client hears from the CTO within an hour.

Speaker notes · slide 8

Every major risk has an owner and a trigger. Our biggest risk is losing the five senior engineers, so they get retention bonuses and a clear role as Release Guardians. Faulty AI code is caught by human sign-off, staged releases and automatic rollback. We manage legal risk with a genuine, well-documented consultation. Client churn is countered by the Guarantee and an executive sponsor for every top-10 client. On cash, if we’re below 1.5 million at month 6 we pause Year-2 hiring automatically. And on culture, the CEO tells staff first, on day one, so they don’t hear it as a rumour — our goal is to rebuild Glassdoor to 3.8 by Year 2.
